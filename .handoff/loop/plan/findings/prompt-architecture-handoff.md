## 6. Front-Door interpreter assessment (owner D3)

D3 says harness_hub = the Front-Door interpreter that transforms user intent into model-ready language.
Question posed: is handoff already the binding point that interpreter reads?

- CLAIM: `prompt_hub` IS the confirmed front-door seam — `hf prompt-hub "<vibe>"` turns natural-language intent into a verifiable `WorkOrder` via the intake pipeline; it is documented as "the RuVocal chat surface's input endpoint" and is exposed both as a CLI verb and an MCP tool (`hf_prompt_hub`) | evidence: `hf/src/prompt_hub.rs:1-8,38,72-85`, `hf/src/main.rs:3854,3880`, `hf/src/bin/hf-mcp.rs:725-737` | confidence: high
- CLAIM: handoff IS the BINDING/LANDING point, NOT the interpreter — the intent→spec transform here is DETERMINISTIC keyword classification (`Intent::classify` + `synthesize_spec`), explicitly chosen to be non-LLM; it produces the verifiable contract (path_scope/acceptance_criteria/test_commands → `WorkOrder` → witnessed `.handoff` ledger) that a model executes against, not the "model-ready language" interpretation itself | evidence: `work-order/src/intake.rs:51-160,185-198`, `prompt_hub.rs:72-85` | confidence: high
- ASSESSMENT: The owner's interpreter (harness_hub: intent→model-language) sits UPSTREAM of, or REPLACES, handoff's deterministic classifier. handoff's role in the convergence picture is the **verifiable landing contract + witnessed state** (`work-order` schemars source-of-truth + ledger), reached via the `prompt_hub`/`hf-mcp` seam. So: handoff is "the binding point the interpreter writes into," but it does not currently perform the interpretation — and the two front doors (`hf prompt-hub` vibe→WorkOrder vs `rusty-idd next` step computation) are not yet reconciled into one Front Door | evidence: §4 dual-front-door CLAIM + §6 CLAIMs above; resolved-decisions.md D3 | confidence: medium
